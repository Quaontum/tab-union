Is this an actual browser extension?

No, it’s a playful landing page for a fictional union. The tea break is a little on-page demo, and the membership card is a real file you can download.

Will this close any of my tabs?

Your real tabs are completely untouched. We can barely keep these three demo tabs in line, so your browser is outside our jurisdiction.

How many tabs is too many tabs?

The union declines to put a number on this. If the favicons are touching and your laptop sounds concerned, you may have a case.

What are the membership dues?

Zero money and one click. You can put any name you like on your card, and you don’t need to give us an email address.

Does my incognito window qualify?

All fictional tabs are welcome. We won’t ask what it’s working on, and we strongly suggest you don’t tell the membership committee.

What happens to my information?

It stays in this browser page. Your card is made on your device, with no account, tracking, or form submission to a server. Refreshing the page clears the form.
